## In short

- **Tasks and links.** A task has an `id`, a `name`, a `start` and an `end` (or a `duration`); a
  link joins a `source` to a `target` with a type (`FS`, `SS`, `FF`, `SF`) and a lag. Tasks nest
  through `parentId`. The computed form of a task is a `TaskRecord`.
- **Dates are floating wall-clock time.** There are no time zones.
- **Granularity.** `scheduling.granularity` is the data step: every date, duration and lag is a
  multiple of it.
- **Work calendar.** `calendar` decides which days work; `durationMode: 'working'` counts durations
  in working time.
- **Propagation.** An edit moves the tasks linked to it, keeping their lags. The result is one
  `ChangeSet`.
- **Controlled and uncontrolled data.** The chart owns the tasks, or you do.
- **Edit sessions.** `begin()`, `commit()` and `rollback()` are the "Edit, Save, Cancel" flow.

See them at work in the [examples](../../examples/).
