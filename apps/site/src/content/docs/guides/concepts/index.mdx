ganttlane has a small number of ideas, and they repeat in every package. A **Task** you pass in
becomes a **TaskRecord** you read back. A drag or an API call becomes one **ChangeSet**. Bad input
becomes an **issue**. This page shows how the pieces fit, which setting applies where, and which
page explains what. Read it once, then use the glossary at the end as an index.

## How data flows

There are two paths through the library, one for reading and one for writing.

```text
READ: your data to the screen

  tasks, links, options
        |   checked at the boundary: bad input is an issue, never a throw
        v
  model: a TaskRecord per task, a LinkRecord per link, rows, issues
        |   work calendar, granularity, hierarchy
        v
  layout: rows, bars and arrows on the TimeScale of the ViewMode
        |
        v
  renderer: draws the visible window and the draft of a drag


WRITE: an edit to your data

  a drag, updateTask(), addLink() ...
        |   propagation along links and to children
        v
  ChangeSet --> before-hook --> cancelled: nothing changes
                    |
                    |  allowed, or rewritten by the hook
                    v
  uncontrolled: the chart applies it to its own copy of the tasks
  controlled:   a draft until you hand back new tasks (patchTasks + setTasks)
                    |
                    v
  events: one tasksChange with every touched task, then taskDatesChange, ...
```

### Reading

You give the chart `tasks`, `links` and options. They are checked once, at the boundary: the shape
of a task with a schema, the rules (unknown references, cycles, the calendar) with plain code. A
problem never throws. It becomes an [issue](./issues/) and the chart repairs or skips the thing: an
invalid task keeps its row without a bar, a bad link is dropped, a bad option takes its default.

What is left is the **model**: a `TaskRecord` per task with its dates as `Date`s, its lengths in
calendar and working days, its progress, its issues and its conflicts, and a `LinkRecord` per link.
Your objects are never changed. From the model the core works out the rows, the bars and the arrows
on the time scale of the current view mode, and the renderer draws only what is on screen.

### Writing

An edit starts as new dates for one task: a drag, or `updateTask`. The edit is then **propagated**:
the tasks linked to it, and the children pinned to it, move so that their links keep their lags. The
result is one `ChangeSet` with every task the edit touched, before and after, and the conflicts of
the result.

The ChangeSet goes through the **before-hook** `onBeforeTaskChange`, which can allow it, cancel it
or rewrite it, also after asking the user. If it is allowed, it is committed. With uncontrolled
data the chart applies it to its own copy of the tasks. With controlled data the chart reports it,
shows it as a draft, and waits for you to hand back the new tasks. Then the events fire: exactly one
`tasksChange` per action.

While a drag is in progress the chart shows a preview of the same computation, as a **draft** over
your data. Nothing is committed, and no hook runs, until the pointer is released.

## Where each setting applies

| Setting                                            | Applies to                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `calendar`, `scheduling.durationMode`              | Reading: working days and working-time lengths of the records. Writing: drags skip days off, lags count working time, conflicts name days off. |
| `scheduling.granularity`, `alignInput`             | Reading: dates, durations and lags are aligned, with a `misaligned` issue. Writing: every ChangeSet is aligned again, hook replacements too.   |
| `scheduling.propagation`, `hierarchy.moveChildren` | Writing only, inside the propagation. They do not change how the data is read.                                                                 |
| `editing`                                          | Gestures from the UI only. Your own calls (`updateTask`, `addLink`, `removeLink`, `setTasks`) are not limited by it.                           |
| `dataMode` (React: `tasks` or `defaultTasks`)      | Writing, at the commit: who applies the change.                                                                                                |
| `history`                                          | Writing: one undo entry per committed action. Off by default.                                                                                  |
| `onBeforeTaskChange`, `onBeforeLinkCreate`         | Writing, before the commit of a drag, an `updateTask` or a new link. Not before undo, redo, rollback or removing a link.                       |

## Glossary

| Term                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                   | Read more                                                                |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Task**            | The object you pass in: an `id`, a `name`, dates, and optional fields. The library never mutates it.                                                      | [Tasks and records](./tasks-and-records/)                                |
| **TaskRecord**      | The computed, read-only form of a task: `Date`s, lengths, progress, `issues`, `conflicts`. From `getTask(id)` or an event.                                | [Tasks and records](./tasks-and-records/)                                |
| **Link**            | A dependency you pass in: a `source`, a `target`, a type and a lag.                                                                                       | [Links and types](./links-and-types/)                                    |
| **LinkRecord**      | The computed form of a link, with its resolved lag and its own issues. From `getLink(id)`.                                                                | [Links and types](./links-and-types/)                                    |
| **WorkCalendar**    | Which days are working days: weekends, holidays, exceptions and your own rule.                                                                            | [Work calendar](./work-calendar/)                                        |
| **TimeScale**       | The linear map between dates and pixels, made from the zoom (`pxPerDay`) of the view mode.                                                                | [`TimeScale`](../../api/ganttlane/core/interfaces/timescale/)            |
| **ViewMode**        | A named scale preset (`'day'`, `'week'`, …): column unit, zoom, header rows and drag snap. You can override presets and add your own.                     | [Options](../options/), [view modes example](../../examples/view-modes/) |
| **Granularity**     | The data step, `'1d'` by default. Every date, duration and lag is a multiple of it.                                                                       | [Granularity and alignment](./granularity-and-alignment/)                |
| **Propagation**     | Moving the tasks linked to an edited task, and its pinned children, so that links keep their lags.                                                        | [Propagation and conflicts](./propagation-and-conflicts/)                |
| **Conflict**        | A warning in a ChangeSet or a record: a bar on a day off, a child outside its parent, a violated link. Never blocks a change.                             | [Propagation and conflicts](./propagation-and-conflicts/#conflicts)      |
| **ChangeSet**       | The outcome of one action: every task it changed, before and after, with the conflicts of the result.                                                     | [Change sets and hooks](./change-sets-and-hooks/)                        |
| **Before-hook**     | `onBeforeTaskChange` and `onBeforeLinkCreate`: your function, run before a commit, that allows, cancels or rewrites it.                                   | [Change sets and hooks](./change-sets-and-hooks/#before-hooks)           |
| **Draft**           | A layer of changes shown over the data: the preview of a drag, a change waiting for a hook, or a change the owner of controlled data has not applied yet. | [Who owns the data](./data-ownership/)                                   |
| **Controlled data** | You own the tasks: the chart reports each change and you hand back the new tasks. The alternative is uncontrolled, the default.                           | [Who owns the data](./data-ownership/)                                   |
| **Edit session**    | A spell of editing with a base to return to: `edit.begin()`, then `commit()` (Save) or `rollback()` (Cancel).                                             | [Edit sessions and history](./edit-sessions-and-history/)                |
| **History**         | Undo and redo, opt in with `history: true`: one entry per committed action.                                                                               | [Edit sessions and history](./edit-sessions-and-history/#undo-and-redo)  |
| **Issue**           | A problem with data or options, reported with a `code` and a `path`. The chart repairs or skips the thing. Never an exception.                            | [Issues instead of exceptions](./issues/)                                |
