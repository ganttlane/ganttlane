All of them are on the object `createGantt` returns (in React: the `ref` of `<Gantt>`, or the
instance of `useGantt`) and need no `this`. The [basic chart](../../examples/basic/) calls
`setViewMode` and `scrollToTask`; the [edit session](../../examples/edit-session/) uses
`edit.begin()`, `commit()` and `rollback()`.
