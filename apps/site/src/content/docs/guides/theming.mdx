The `theme` option is `'auto'` (follow `prefers-color-scheme`, the default), `'light'` or `'dark'`.
The stylesheet is in `@layer gantt`, so any style of your own wins without `!important`: the
[release plan](../../examples/release-plan/) colours its bars by status with one custom property per
class. The charts on this site follow the theme of the site.
